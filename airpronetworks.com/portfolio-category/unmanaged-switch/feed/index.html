Power down Idle Ports:-

When a computer or network equipment is off, the corresponding port of a traditional switch will continue to consume considerable amounts of power. The IT can automatically detect the link status of each port and reduce the power consumption of ports that are idle.

Power Budget According to Cable Length:-
